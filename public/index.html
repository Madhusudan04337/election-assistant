The 12 Steps to Voting

1 / 12

Election Timeline

Here's what happens at each stage of the election process:

1

Election Announcement

The government announces that elections will be held. Dates are set.

2

Nomination Period

Candidates submit their nominations to run in the elections.

3

Campaign Period

Candidates campaign to convince voters to vote for them.

4

Polling Day

Voting booths open and voters cast their votes.

5

Vote Counting

Election officials count all votes in a secure, transparent process.

6

Results Declaration

Official results are announced and winners are declared.

About This Guide

Why This Guide?

This voter education guide is designed to help first-time voters in India understand the complete voting process in simple, easy-to-understand language.

Who Is This For?

  • First-time voters (18+ years old)
  • Students learning about democracy
  • Anyone who wants to understand voting better
  • Parents and teachers guiding young voters

What You'll Learn

  • Check if you're eligible to vote

  • What to bring to the polling booth
  • Step-by-step voting process
  • How votes are counted
  • Common questions and answers
