# Complete Guide to Steps 3-11: Election Learning Flow

## ✓ STEP 3: VERIFY VOTER DETAILS
### "Check Your Voter Information"

#### 1-Line Summary
"Make sure your name, address, and polling booth information are correct in the voter database before election day."

#### Key Content

**What You Need to Check:**

```
✓ Your Full Name
  - Check if it's spelled correctly
  - Should match your ID documents

✓ Your Address  
  - Current residential address
  - Area/ward/constituency name
  - PIN code

✓ Your EPIC Number (Voter ID)
  - 10-digit unique number
  - Will be printed on your voter card

✓ Polling Booth Location
  - Building name
  - Street address
  - Booth number
```

#### How to Verify (Two Methods)

**Method 1: Online (Fastest)**
- Visit: https://www.nvsp.in
- Click "Check Electoral Roll"
- Select your state and district
- Search by name or EPIC number
- Check all details carefully

**Method 2: Visit Election Office**
- Go to your nearest Voter Registration Office
- Staff will help you find your name
- Takes 10-15 minutes
- Get a printout if needed

#### Common Mistakes to Avoid

```
❌ MISTAKE 1: Wrong Spelling
- Name spelled differently? (e.g., "Raj" vs "Rajesh")
- Contact election office to correct it

❌ MISTAKE 2: Old Address
- Moved to new house/city?
- Address not updated? 
- File Form 8C to change address

❌ MISTAKE 3: Name Not Found
- Not in voter list even though eligible?
- Register immediately using Form 6
- Takes 3-4 weeks to process

❌ MISTAKE 4: Wrong Polling Booth
- Booth location might have changed
- Always double-check on election day
- Don't assume same booth as last time
```

#### What to Do Next
"Write down your polling booth location and keep it safe. Visit in person if anything looks wrong. You have time to fix it before election day!"

## 📅 STEP 4: ELECTION SCHEDULE
### "Understanding When Each Stage Happens"

#### 1-Line Summary
"Elections happen in several stages. Learn the timeline so you know what's happening when."

#### Election Timeline (In Order)

```
STAGE 1: ANNOUNCEMENT
├─ Date: Announced 3-4 months before voting
├─ What Happens: Election Commission announces voting date
├─ What You Do: Check if you're registered
└─ Time to Act: Register now if not on voter list!

STAGE 2: NOMINATION
├─ Date: 1.5-2 months before voting
├─ What Happens: Candidates file nomination papers
├─ What You Do: Find out who's running in your area
└─ Time to Act: Research candidates

STAGE 3: CAMPAIGN
├─ Date: 1-1.5 months before voting
├─ What Happens: Candidates visit your area, give speeches
├─ What You Do: Attend meetings, learn about candidates
└─ Time to Act: Listen and decide who you want to vote for

STAGE 4: POLLING DAY (THE VOTING DAY!)
├─ Date: Announced in Stage 1
├─ What Happens: You go vote at your polling booth
├─ What You Do: Vote between 7 AM - 6 PM
└─ Time to Act: This is the important day! Plan to be free

STAGE 5: COUNTING
├─ Date: Usually next day after voting
├─ What Happens: Election officials count all votes
├─ What You Do: Wait for results
└─ Time to Act: You can watch counting on TV or at election office

STAGE 6: RESULT DECLARATION
├─ Date: Same day or next day as counting
├─ What Happens: Winner is announced
├─ What You Do: Accept the results peacefully
└─ Time to Act: Democracy is complete!
```

#### Easy Way to Remember
```
Think of it like a sports tournament:
- Announcement = Tournament announced
- Nomination = Teams sign up
- Campaign = Teams practice and promote
- Polling = The match day
- Counting = Counting the points
- Results = Winner declared
```

#### Why Each Stage Matters
- **Announcement**: Gives you time to register
- **Nomination**: You see who's running
- **Campaign**: You learn about candidates and issues
- **Polling**: This is YOUR power day
- **Counting**: Fair and transparent process
- **Results**: Final decision announced

#### What to Do Next
"Mark the polling date in your calendar now! Don't wait until the last day."

#### First-Timer Tip
"Elections are planned months in advance. The government gives plenty of time for everyone to prepare. Check the official website regularly for updates."

#### CTA Button Label
**"See Full Election Schedule →"** or **"Mark Polling Date in Calendar"**

#### FAQ Question
**Q: "If I miss the nomination stage, can I still vote?"**  
A: "Yes! The nomination stage is for candidates. You can vote in the polling stage no matter what. Missing nomination doesn't affect you as a voter."

#### Quiz Question
**Q: "In what order do these election stages happen?"**
- Polling, Campaign, Nomination, Announcement | **Announcement, Nomination, Campaign, Polling ✓** | Campaign, Nomination, Announcement, Polling | Polling, Nomination, Campaign, Announcement

## 🆔 STEP 7: IDENTITY VERIFICATION AT THE BOOTH
### "Getting Your Name Checked"

#### 1-Line Summary
"When it's your turn, officials will verify your identity to make sure you're you and you're on the voter list."

#### What Happens: Step by Step

```
STEP 1: YOU REACH THE COUNTER
├─ What You'll See: Small desk with official and laptop
├─ What They Say: "Good, next voter!" or "Please come"
├─ What You Do: Walk calmly to the counter
└─ How You Feel: Nervous? Normal! Breathe. ✓

STEP 2: OFFICIAL ASKS YOUR NAME
├─ What They Ask: "What's your name?" or "Your name, please?"
├─ What You Do: Say your name clearly, not too fast
├─ What They Check: Official looks in voter list on laptop
└─ How Long: 10-15 seconds

STEP 3: OFFICIAL CHECKS YOUR ID
├─ What They Ask: "Can I see your ID?"
├─ What You Do: Politely hand over your valid ID
├─ What They Check: Your photo, name, and address match
├─ What They Do: Scan or note your ID number
└─ Time: 10-15 seconds

STEP 4: OFFICIAL VERIFIES YOU
├─ What They Do: Cross-check your details in the system
├─ What They Look For: Do they match? Are you on the list?
├─ What They Might Ask: "Is your address correct?" "Is this your current phone number?"
├─ What You Do: Answer truthfully and calmly
└─ Time: 20-30 seconds

STEP 5: OFFICIAL SAYS "YOU'RE GOOD!"
├─ What They Say: "You're on the list" or "You can vote"
├─ What They Do: Mark your name as "VOTED"
├─ What You Feel: Relief! You passed verification!
└─ Next: Move to finger inking
```

## 🚪 STEP 10: CAST THE VOTE
### "How to Actually Vote"

#### 1-Line Summary
"Inside the voting booth, you'll mark your choice on a ballot or press a button on the electronic machine—it's simple, secret, and final."

#### How Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) Works

```
ELECTRONIC VOTING MACHINE VOTING:

YOUR ACTIONS:
1. LOOK at the machine
   - You see candidate names/symbols
   - Red panel with buttons (one per candidate)

2. READ carefully
   - Read all candidates from top to bottom
   - Take your time
   - Make sure you recognize your choice

3. FIND your choice
   - Look for the name you want to vote for
   - Remember the symbol (e.g., lotus, hand, etc.)
   - Make sure it's the right candidate

4. PRESS the button
   - Press the red button next to your choice
   - You'll see light blink next to it
   - Don't press hard - gentle press is enough

5. CONFIRM your choice
   - A light flashes confirming your selection
   - Wait 2-3 seconds
   - You'll hear a beep (different machines beep differently)

6. VOTE IS CAST!
   - Your vote is recorded in the machine
   - The machine is now locked
   - You cannot change it
   - It's final and secure

7. COME OUT of booth
   - Curtain automatically opens
   - Or you push it open
   - You're done!
```

#### How Ballot Paper Works

```
BALLOT PAPER VOTING:

YOUR ACTIONS:
1. RECEIVE ballot paper
   - Official gives you blank ballot
   - Fold it so others can't see
   - Go into booth with the pen they give

2. UNFOLD and READ
   - All candidates listed with symbols
   - Read from top to bottom
   - Check names and symbols carefully

3. MARK your choice
   - Use the BLACK pen given by officials
   - Mark an X or checkmark next to ONE name
   - Mark clearly and firmly
   - Not too light (must be visible)
   - Not too hard (shouldn't tear paper)

4. MARK ONLY ONE
   - One candidate only!
   - Not two, not three - ONE ONLY
   - If you mark two, your vote becomes invalid
   - So be VERY careful

5. FOLD the paper
   - Fold so ballot doesn't show
   - Keep it folded when coming out
   - Don't let anyone see it

6. GIVE to official
   - Hand to ballot officer
   - They put it in the sealed box
   - Your vote is recorded

7. IT'S FINAL
   - You can't get it back
   - Your vote is now with thousands of others
   - Completely mixed up (that's good!)
```
