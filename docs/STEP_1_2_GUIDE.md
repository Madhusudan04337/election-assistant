## **STEP 1: ELIGIBILITY CHECK**

### **Section Heading**
**Are You Eligible to Vote? Find Out!**

### **One-Line Summary**
Before you can vote, you need to check if you meet the requirements to be a voter in India.

### **Key Requirements (Bullet Points)**

✅ **You must be:**
- A citizen of India
- At least 18 years old
- A resident of the area where you want to vote

❌ **You cannot vote if you are:**
- Non-citizen of India
- Under 18 years old
- Have been convicted of certain serious crimes
- Not a resident of the area
- Have been legally disqualified from voting

### **"What to Do Next" Line**
"Check the requirements above. If you meet all of them, you're ready! Move to the next step to learn how to register as a voter."

### **Tip for First-Time Voters**
**💡 Quick Tip:** You don't need to own property, have a job, or be rich to vote! Age, citizenship, and living in the area are the only things that matter. If you're 18 and an Indian citizen, you can vote!

### **FAQ Question for Step 1**
**Q: Do I need to own property in the area to vote there?**

**A:** No! You only need to live (reside) in the area. You don't need to own a house or any property. If you rent a room, stay in a hostel, or live with family, that counts as residence. The Election Commission just wants to make sure you have a connection to the area.

---

### **Quiz Question for Step 1**

**Question:** What is the minimum age to vote in India?

**Options:**
- A) 16 years old
- B) **18 years old** ✓ (Correct Answer)
- C) 21 years old

**Explanation:** You must be at least 18 years old to be eligible to register as a voter in India. This is the same age when you become an adult legally in India.

## **STEP 2: VOTER REGISTRATION**

### **Section Heading**
**Register as a Voter: Your Path to Voting**

### **One-Line Summary**
Voter registration is the official process of adding your name to the voter list so you can vote in elections.

### **Key Points (Bullet Points)**

✅ **Why Register?**
- Your name needs to be in the official voter list to vote
- Registration creates your official voter record
- Without registration, you cannot vote, even if you're eligible

📱 **Two Ways to Register:**
1. **Online (Fastest):** Visit the Election Commission website and fill the form from home
2. **Offline (In-Person):** Visit your local Voter Registration Office (VRO) with documents

📋 **What You'll Need:**
- Aadhar Card (best option)
- PAN Card
- Passport
- Driving License
- Or any proof of residence (utility bill, college ID)

### **"What to Do Next" Line**
"Choose the method that's easiest for you—online or offline. Fill in your details correctly and upload/attach your documents. You'll get confirmation via email or SMS once your registration is approved!"

### **Tip for First-Time Voters**
**💡 Quick Tip:** The **online method is fastest and easiest** — you can do it in 5-10 minutes from your phone or computer! You can register from home, and the Election Commission will send you a confirmation via SMS or email. No need to wait in lines!

### **FAQ Question for Step 2**
**Q: My name is already on the voter list. Do I need to register again?**

**A:** No! If your name is already on the voter list, you don't need to register again. You can skip this step and go to Step 3 to find your voter ID. But if your name is NOT on the list and you're eligible, follow the registration steps above to register now.

### **Detailed Information for Website Display**

#### **Step 2A: If Name Is Already Registered**
- ✅ You're in luck! Your voter registration is already done
- 🚀 Skip to Step 3: Check Your Voter Details
- 📋 You might have received a voter card in the mail, or you can find your details online

#### **Step 2B: If Name Is NOT in the Voter List**
- 📝 You need to register now
- 🌐 Online registration takes 5-10 minutes
- 🏛️ Or visit your nearest VRO office
- ⏰ Registration usually takes 1-2 weeks to process
- 📱 You'll get confirmation via SMS/email

#### **Common Registration Questions**
1. **"When can I register?"** → Anytime, year-round! The Election Commission accepts registrations 365 days a year.

2. **"How long does it take?"** → Online confirmation takes 1-2 weeks. VRO registration can take 3-4 weeks. Be patient!

3. **"What if I move to a new city?"** → You can register again in your new city. You can only vote in ONE place, so choose where you currently live.

4. **"Do I need to bring anything if I register online?"** → No! Everything is digital. Just have a photo of your ID document ready to upload.

5. **"My Aadhar has a typo. Can I still use it?"** → Yes! Minor typos are usually okay. But if there's a major issue, use a different document like PAN or Passport.
