# Interactive Learning Card Template for UX Writers

## Overview
This template helps UX writers create beginner-friendly, mobile-first learning cards for any step of the voting process. Each card includes all essential information in simple language.

## Template 1: Eligibility Check Card Example

### Title
"Step 1: Are You Eligible to Vote?"

### Icon Suggestion
- **Icon**: Checkmark or ID card
- **Color**: Primary blue (#0066CC)
- **Style**: Solid, 48px size

### One-Line Summary
"Check if you meet the requirements to be a voter in India."

### Short Explanation
To vote in India, you need to be eligible. Eligibility is simple—you just need to meet a few basic requirements. If you do, you can vote in elections!

### Key Points (Bullet Points)
```
You CAN vote if you:
✓ Are a citizen of India
✓ Are 18 years or older
✓ Live in the voting area

You CANNOT vote if you:
✗ Are not an Indian citizen
✗ Are under 18 years old
✗ Have been convicted of serious crimes
```

### First-Timer Tip (Tip Box)
💡 "You don't need to own property or have a job to vote! Age, citizenship, and living in the area are the only things that matter."

### FAQ Question & Answer
**Q: Can I vote if I'm 17 but turning 18 before the election?**  
**A:** No. You must be 18 years old on voting day. If you turn 18 after voting day, you can vote in the next election.

### Quiz Question
**Q: At what age can you start voting in India?**
- 16 years old
- 18 years old ✓ (Correct)
- 21 years old
- 25 years old

### CTA Button Label
Primary: **"I'm Eligible - Continue →"**  
Secondary: **"Check Requirements"**

### Title
"Step 2: Register as a Voter"

- **Icon**: Clipboard or pen
- **Color**: Green accent (#00A651)
- **Style**: Solid, 48px size

### One-Line Summary
"Add your name to the official voters list so you can vote."

### Short Explanation
Before you can vote, your name must be on the official voters list. Registration is the process of adding your name to this list. It's free and takes just 5 minutes online!

### Key Points
```
Why Register?
✓ Your name must be on the voters list
✓ Without registration, you cannot vote
✓ Registration is FREE

Two Ways to Register:
✓ Online (Fastest - 5-10 minutes)
✓ Offline at Voter Registration Office (3-4 weeks)

Documents Needed (Pick ANY ONE):
✓ Aadhar Card
✓ PAN Card
✓ Passport
✓ Driving License
```

### First-Timer Tip
💡 "Register online from your phone—it takes just 5 minutes! You don't need to go to any office. Just fill your details and upload a photo of your ID."

### FAQ Question & Answer
**Q: My name is already on the voters list. Do I need to register again?**  
**A:** No! If your name is already registered, you're all set. Just skip to the next step.

### Quiz Question
**Q: What's the quickest way to register as a voter?**
- Visit the Voter Registration Office in person
- Register online ✓ (Correct)
- Call the Election Commission
- Ask your family member to register for you

## Template 3: Voting Area Card Example

### Title
"Step 9: Enter Your Private Voting Booth"

### Icon Suggestion
- **Icon**: Lock or booth icon
- **Color**: Green (#00A651)
- **Style**: Solid, 48px size

### One-Line Summary
"Move to the private voting compartment where you'll cast your vote in complete secrecy."

### Short Explanation
This is the moment you've been waiting for! You're about to cast your vote in private. No one can see how you vote. Your choice is completely secret—this is what makes voting fair and free.

### Key Points
```
When You Enter the Booth:
✓ You are completely alone
✓ No one can watch you vote
✓ Polling staff wait outside
✓ Your vote is 100% secret

What to Expect:
✓ A closed compartment with a curtain or walls
✓ A voting machine (EVM) or ballot paper
✓ A way to mark or register your choice
✓ No one sees your vote

Remember:
✓ Take your time—no rush
✓ Mark or press carefully
✓ Your vote is yours alone
✓ No one will ever know who you voted for
```

### First-Timer Tip
💡 "Don't be nervous! This booth is your private space. Take a few seconds, make your choice calmly, and remember—what you do here is 100% secret. That's the beauty of democracy!"

### FAQ Question & Answer
**Q: What if I don't know who to vote for when I'm in the booth?**  
**A:** That's okay. Take your time and think. You can ask for information about candidates before entering the booth. The polling staff can help. There's no time limit—vote when you're ready.

### Quiz Question
**Q: Can anyone see how you vote in the voting booth?**
- Yes, polling staff can watch
- No, voting is 100% secret ✓ (Correct)
- Yes, election observers watch
- Yes, election officials see everything

## Writing Guidelines for UX Writers

### Language
- Use simple, everyday English
- Avoid jargon and technical terms
- Use short sentences (under 15 words)
- Use active voice ("You mark the ballot" not "The ballot is marked")
- Address readers directly ("You" and "Your")

### Tone
- Friendly and encouraging
- Respectful and neutral
- Reassuring (address fears)
- Empowering (highlight reader's power)
- Non-judgmental

### Structure
- Start with the most important info
- Use bullet points for scannability
- Break up text into small chunks
- Use icons and visual hierarchy
- Keep mobile-first—short text wins

### Content Principles
- One idea per bullet point
- Every sentence earns its place
- FAQ answers are 1-2 sentences max
- Tips are short and actionable
- Quiz questions have one clear right answer

### Mobile-First Tips
- Stack content vertically
- Use larger fonts (16px+ body text)
- Wide tap targets (44px minimum)
- Short headings (3-5 words)
- Break sections with white space

### Accessibility
- Use semantic HTML headings
- Add alt text to all icons
- Use high contrast colors
- Make buttons descriptive
- Test with screen readers

## Quick Checklist for UX Writers

- [ ] Title is clear and action-oriented
- [ ] One-liner summarizes the step in 10 words or less
- [ ] Explanation is 2-3 sentences maximum
- [ ] 3-5 bullet points (not more)
- [ ] Tip addresses a common fear or misconception
- [ ] FAQ is a real question people ask
- [ ] Quiz has one obviously correct answer
- [ ] Language is simple (high school level)
- [ ] Mobile layout is vertical (single column)
- [ ] CTA buttons are clear and direct
- [ ] Content is neutral and unbiased
- [ ] No jargon or technical terms
