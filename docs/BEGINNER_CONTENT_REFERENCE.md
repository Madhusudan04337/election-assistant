# Beginner-Friendly Content Reference Guide
## Steps 1 & 2: Eligibility & Registration

---

## **STEP 1: ELIGIBILITY CHECK**

### Quick Facts
| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **What It Is** | Check if you meet the requirements to be a voter |
| **Key Age** | Must be 18+ years old |
| **Citizenship** | Must be Indian citizen |
| **Residence** | Must live in the voting area |
| **Time to Check** | 2-3 minutes |

### Exact Content for Website

#### Section Heading
```
Are You Eligible to Vote? Find Out!
```

#### One-Line Summary
```
Before you can vote, you need to check if you meet the requirements to be a voter in India.
```

#### Three Key Requirements (Bullet Points)
```
✅ You must be:
• A citizen of India
• At least 18 years old
• A resident of the area where you want to vote
```

#### Things That Disqualify (What NOT Required)
```
❌ You CANNOT vote if you are:
• Non-citizen of India
• Under 18 years old
• Convicted of certain serious crimes
• Not a resident of the area
• Legally disqualified from voting
```

#### "What to Do Next" CTA Line
```
Check the requirements above. If you meet all of them, you're ready! 
Move to the next step to learn how to register as a voter.
```

#### First-Timer Tip (Highlight Box)
```
💡 Quick Tip: 
You don't need to own property, have a job, or be rich to vote! 
Age, citizenship, and living in the area are the only things that matter. 
If you're 18 and an Indian citizen, you can vote!
```

#### CTA Button Label
```
"I'm Eligible - Let's Register →"
```

### FAQ for Step 1

**Q: Do I need to own property in the area to vote there?**

A: No! You only need to live (reside) in the area. You don't need to own a house or any property. If you rent a room, stay in a hostel, or live with family, that counts as residence. The Election Commission just wants to make sure you have a connection to the area.

### Quiz Question for Step 1

**Question:** What is the minimum age to vote in India?

**Options:**
- A) 16 years old
- B) **18 years old** ✅ CORRECT
- C) 21 years old
- D) 25 years old

**Explanation (for when user selects answer):**
```
Correct! You must be at least 18 years old to be eligible to register 
as a voter in India. This is the same age when you become an adult 
legally in India.
```

## **STEP 2: VOTER REGISTRATION**

### Quick Facts
| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **What It Is** | Official enrollment to the voter list |
| **Online Time** | 5-10 minutes |
| **Offline Time** | 3-4 weeks to process |
| **Cost** | Completely FREE |
| **Best Method** | Online (fastest) |

### Exact Content for Website

#### Section Heading
```
Register as a Voter: Your Path to Voting
```

#### One-Line Summary
```
Voter registration is the official process of adding your name to the 
voter list so you can vote in elections.
```

#### Key Points (Bullet Points)

**Why Register?**
```
• Your name needs to be in the official voter list to vote
• Registration creates your official voter record
• Without registration, you cannot vote, even if you're eligible
```

**Two Ways to Register:**
```
📱 Online (Fastest):
   Visit the Election Commission website and fill the form from home

🏛️ Offline (In-Person):
   Visit your local Voter Registration Office (VRO) with documents
```

**What You'll Need (Any ONE of these):**
```
• Aadhar Card (BEST - most accepted)
• PAN Card
• Passport
• Driving License
• Proof of residence (utility bill, college ID)
```

#### "What to Do Next" CTA Line
```
Choose the method that's easiest for you—online or offline. 
Fill in your details correctly and upload/attach your documents. 
You'll get confirmation via email or SMS once your registration is approved!
```

#### First-Timer Tip (Highlight Box)
```
💡 Quick Tip:
The ONLINE method is fastest and easiest — you can do it in 5-10 minutes 
from your phone! Do it from home, and you'll get confirmation via SMS. 
No waiting in lines!
```

#### CTA Button Labels
```
Primary: "Start Online Registration →"
Secondary: "Go to VRO Office"
```

### Online Registration Steps

```
Step 1: Visit Election Commission of India website
Step 2: Click on "Register to Vote" or "Voter Registration"
Step 3: Fill the online form with your details
Step 4: Upload proof document (Aadhar, PAN, etc.)
Step 5: Submit the application
Step 6: Get confirmation via email or SMS
```

### Offline Registration Steps

```
Step 1: Find your nearest Voter Registration Office (VRO)
Step 2: Collect Form 6 (registration form)
Step 3: Fill it with your details and sign
Step 4: Attach proof of citizenship and residence
Step 5: Submit to the VRO officer
Step 6: Keep the receipt for tracking
```

### FAQ for Step 2

**Q: My name is already on the voter list. Do I need to register again?**

A: No! If your name is already on the voter list, you don't need to register again. You can skip this step and go to Step 3 to find your voter ID. But if your name is NOT on the list and you're eligible, follow the registration steps above to register now.

### Quiz Question for Step 2

**Question:** What is the quickest way to register as a voter?

**Options:**
- A) Visit the VRO office in person
- B) **Register online through the official website** ✅ CORRECT
- C) Call the Election Commission office
- D) Ask a family member to register for you

**Explanation (for when user selects answer):**
```
Correct! Online registration is the fastest way. You can complete it 
in 5-10 minutes from home and get confirmation via SMS or email. 
No need to visit an office or wait in lines!
```

## **RESPONSIVE DESIGN**

### Desktop (1024px+)
- Two columns for major sections
- Side-by-side cards
- Full-width content

### Tablet (768px - 1023px)
- Two columns still work
- Slightly smaller padding
- Readable font sizes

### Mobile (< 768px)
- Single column (stack everything)
- Full-width cards
- Larger tap targets (44px+ buttons)
- Readable font sizes (16px+ for body)
