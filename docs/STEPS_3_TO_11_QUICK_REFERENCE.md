# Quick Reference: Steps 3-11 - One Page Summary

## Step 3: Verify Voter Details
**Do:** Check name, address, EPIC number, polling booth online or at election office  
**Avoid:** Wrong spelling, outdated address, assuming booth location  
**Time:** 5-10 minutes  
**CTA:** "Check My Details Online →"

## Step 4: Election Schedule  
**Timeline:** Announcement → Nomination → Campaign → Polling → Counting → Results  
**Key:** Each stage is important  
**Remember:** You have months to prepare  
**CTA:** "See Full Schedule →"

---

## Step 5: Prepare Before Day
**Checklist:**
- [ ] Mark polling date on calendar
- [ ] Find booth location (online or office)
- [ ] Gather valid ID
- [ ] Plan route and time
- [ ] Get good sleep night before

**Best time:** 7-9 AM (least crowded)  
**CTA:** "Download Checklist →"

---

## Step 6: Reach the Booth
**Actions:**
1. Find your booth using Google Maps
2. Look for "POLLING BOOTH" signboard
3. Stand in line peacefully
4. Wait for your turn

**Expect:** 15-30 minute wait total  
**Tip:** Early morning has shortest queue  
**CTA:** "Get Booth Location →"

---

## Step 7: Identity Verification
**Process:**
1. Tell official your name
2. Show your valid ID
3. Official checks against voter list
4. If verified → move to ink station
5. If not found → fill Form 7 (special vote)

**Don't worry:** You have the right to be there  
**Tip:** Stay calm, it takes only 60 seconds  
**CTA:** "Next: Finger Inking →"

---

## Step 8: Finger Inking & Register Entry
**What happens:**
1. Get finger marked with special ink (safe, non-toxic)
2. Receive voter slip (keep as memory)
3. Sign in official register next to your name

**Time:** 15-20 seconds total  
**Ink:** Safe, fades in 1-2 days  
**Tip:** The ink prevents voting twice (fraud prevention)  
**CTA:** "Ready for Voting →"

---

## Step 9: Move to Voting Area
**Actions:**
1. Show your inked finger (final check)
2. Enter private voting booth
3. Close curtain
4. You're now alone and private

**Remember:** 100% secret, no one watching  
**Time:** 2-3 minutes to vote  
**CTA:** "I'm Ready to Vote →"

---

## Step 10: Cast Your Vote
**EVM (Machine):**
1. Read all candidates
2. Press red button next to your choice
3. See light blink confirming choice
4. Press CONFIRM button
5. Hear beep - vote is recorded!

**Ballot Paper:**
1. Read all candidates
2. Mark ONE with provided pen
3. Fold so choice doesn't show
4. Give to official
5. Vote recorded in sealed box

**Remember:** Only ONE choice! Keep it secret!  
**Time:** 2-3 minutes  
**CTA:** "How to Vote →"

---

## Step 11: Leave the Station
**After Voting:**
- Walk out peacefully
- Don't tell anyone who you voted for
- Be proud of your inked finger
- Encourage others to vote
- Celebrate your participation in democracy!

**Remember:** Your vote is safe, secret, and counted  
**What to Share:** "I voted!" (not who you voted for)  
**CTA:** "I Voted! ✓"

## Key Facts

| Fact | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Age** | Must be 18+ to vote |
| **Residence** | Must live in the area (rent counts!) |
| **Time** | Booths open 7 AM - 6 PM |
| **ID** | Bring any ONE valid government ID |
| **Ink** | Safe, special voting ink, fades in 1-2 days |
| **Secrecy** | 100% secret, no one knows your choice |
| **Invalid Votes** | Marking 2 people = vote doesn't count |
